## 🎯 Design Philosophy

### Professional Elements:
- **Clean typography** - Playfair Display (serif) + Inter (sans-serif)
- **Generous whitespace** - Not cluttered
- **Subtle animations** - Professional, not distracting
- **Photo-driven layout** - Real moments over icons

### Brand Colors (From Logo):
- Pink gradient: `#ec4899 → #db2777`
- Purple gradient: `#a855f7 → #7c3aed`
- Gold accent: `#fbbf24 → #f59e0b`

### Layout Style:
- **Editorial magazine feel** - Not template-like
- **Asymmetric grids** - More interesting than 50/50
- **Large typography** - Bold headlines
- **Photo integration** - Images are part of storytelling
