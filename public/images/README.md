## Image Specifications

### Recommended Sizes:
- **Hero Image**: 600×700px (portrait)
- **Manifesto**: 700×500px (landscape)
- **Team Photo**: 600×700px (portrait)
- **GES 2017**: 500×350px (landscape)
- **Focus Areas**: 400×300px (landscape)

### Format:
- **File Type**: JPG or PNG
- **Quality**: High resolution (72-150 DPI for web)
- **File Size**: Keep under 500KB for fast loading (use compression)

### Optimization Tools:
- TinyPNG (https://tinypng.com/) - Free compression
- Squoosh (https://squoosh.app/) - Google's image optimizer
- ImageOptim (Mac) or RIOT (Windows)

## How to Replace

1. **Save your photos** with exact filenames listed above
2. **Place them** in `public/images/` directory
3. **Refresh the page** - Next.js will automatically load them
4. **Check responsiveness** on mobile/tablet

## Photo Tips for Best Results

✅ **DO:**
- Use high-quality, well-lit photos
- Show real people, real moments
- Include diversity in representation
- Capture emotion and energy
- Use professional event photography

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use stock photos
- Include blurry or low-res images
- Show empty venues or staged shots
- Use heavily filtered/edited photos
- Include outdated branding
